## Definitions

- **cagr:** Annualized compounded net portfolio return; monthly panels report trailing-12-month and expanding-to-date estimates after at least 12 observations.
- **sharpe:** sqrt(12) times mean monthly net excess return over reporting T-bill divided by sample standard deviation.
- **rank_ic:** Each month, cross-sectional Spearman correlation of available pod scores and observed next-month excess stock returns. Missing realized returns excluded. Summary is unweighted mean across 68 monthly ICs.
- **rank_ic_common_eligible:** Same statistic on the intersection of stocks covered by all five score sets and satisfying both long and short formation eligibility at the COMBINED book capital. The compared stock set is identical across all books each month, after excluding missing realized returns.
- **max_dd:** Minimum month-end NAV / running peak NAV - 1, including the initial NAV. Rolling drawdown resets the peak at the beginning of each trailing 12-month window.
- **alpha_ann:** CAPM intercept from monthly net portfolio excess return regressed on S&P 500 price-index excess return, multiplied by 12.
- **alpha4_ann:** Intercept also controlling for runtime panel-built size, value and momentum factors, multiplied by 12. Not official Fama-French factor series.
- **pods:** Separate standalone books, each endowed with $1bn and sized with its own causal compounding path. Same current Treasury/turnover/exposure/risk/cost rules. No graph overlay in standalone pods; combined retains graph. Not additive portfolio attribution.
- **annual:** Calendar-year total returns, not CAGR. 2026 has eight observations. Annual alpha from only 8-12 observations is noisy.
- **limits:** Monthly results. Existing missing daily held returns and daily/monthly reconciliation failures mean daily volatility and submission readiness remain uncertified.
