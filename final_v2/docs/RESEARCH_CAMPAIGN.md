# Connected research campaign

Run `python final_v2/run.py` in the project environment. The root configuration selects `research_campaign`; every other entrypoint is optional. Run `python final_v2/verify_campaign.py` to reload and replay the latest campaign's recorded orders independently.

The campaign connects dated paper discovery, approved provider receipts, all supplied characteristics, local 8-K text measurements, agent-written Python, protected evaluation, measured feedback, permanent pod ownership, continuous capital allocation, staged fills, daily NAV and competition reporting. Theo's saved portfolio and fitted models are not imported.

1. Read only authorized development observations. Preserve missingness and distinguish the full research universe from the fund's 200–500 position constraint.
2. Search paper versions dated by the development cutoff, retain their actual text and identifiers, and discover provider metadata within durable request limits. A provider receipt or a publication timestamp alone does not certify historical vintages or issuer mappings.
3. Compute dated filing counts, transparent literal text/event measurements, and any agent-proposed literal phrase measurements locally. Raw filing text is not transmitted automatically. Unseen issuer coverage remains unknown. The supplied filing corpus's survivorship limitation remains explicit.
4. Ask the configured model for executable numerical Python, a mechanism, falsification and timing rules, one pod at a time. There is no requirement to reuse a previous proposal. Request and response hashes, actual provider identity and all failures are retained. New calls are paced and charged against the persistent shared account; reopening does not reset usage.
5. Execute each strategy using only current/past ranked features, up to twelve historical monthly snapshots and up to 252 past daily returns. Imports, IO, credentials, arbitrary calls, new array dimensions and evaluation outcomes are excluded by the numerical language. The model cannot edit its evaluator or approve its own performance.
6. Measure the full concentration curve before capital. Choose broad supported concentration regions using prior settled observations; use the fund's maximum name count as a declared construction ceiling when no region has sufficient evidence. A default research construction does not establish an edge.
7. Send compact measured feedback back to the programmer for revision. Retain rejected and null hypotheses in the attempt ledger. A contemporary research loop over all development observations is not an untouched historical validation.
8. Keep one to three research strategies in each pod. Select ownership on capital-free gross evidence and diversification. Separately subtract fixed spread/borrow costs and uncertainty, then solve a continuous marginal-edge allocation subject to fund, pod, liquidity and modeled borrow limits. Unused capital remains cash.
9. Preserve native daily, weekly or slower signal reviews. Monthly CIO reviews do not force every sleeve to refresh its forecast. Orders execute at the following session's close and earn returns subsequently; participation and order expiry can stage fills over multiple sessions.
10. Post cash interest inside the execution NAV, charge realized modeled trading/borrow costs, save actual holdings and sparse target instructions, and replay the saved instructions before publishing a research report.

Artifacts live under the configured private state directory. `latest_campaign.json` points to the immutable-input campaign directory. This contains source snapshots, input digests, evidence, generated `.py` files, every attempt, feedback, pod books, capital allocations, daily/monthly ledgers, actual holdings and a frozen research book. The independent verifier checks input hashes, timing, ownership, capacity, holdings, daily P&L and final NAV.

Headline reporting separates arithmetic annualized return from CAGR, Sharpe against cash from information ratio against TB3MS+4%, and monthly from daily statistics. S&P excess-return regressions report observed coverage and standard errors; missing licensed history is not fabricated. The explicitly configured FRED container is projected and filtered to reporting series only. Other legacy external rows are never alpha inputs.

A completed run means the connected experiment executed. Financial admission is a separate conclusion: constraints, costs, stability, data provenance, attempt multiplicity and untouched evaluation must support it. Nonempty research rosters and a successful replay do not certify alpha. Borrow inventory and fees remain disclosed proxies until actual historical quotes are supplied.
