@@ campaign.selection_score
Candidate ownership and refinement use S=0.40P+0.25C+0.20R+0.15B. Components
are in [0,1]. P=clip(t_IC/3,0,1), where t_IC uses monthly rank IC with a
Newey-West error accounting for the declared horizon's overlapping labels.
C is the fraction of valid months with IC>0. R=clip(0.5+mean_IC_12m/(2*0.05),0,1)
over the latest twelve calendar months of settled evidence. B is the fraction
of performed predefined perturbation checks with positive mean IC. The checks
shift the label start by one session, use 80% and 120% of the declared horizon,
and trim the outer 5% of active forecast scores at each end. They never change
the declared stock cohort or select a favorable direction after seeing results.
Only active nonzero forecasts are ranked against contemporaneous industry/size
peers inside the declared universe. Missing or unsettled outcomes cannot pass a
check. No performed checks gives B=0 and an explicit missing-evidence flag.
The minimum valid-month rule controls ranking eligibility; the score has no
extra t-statistic or return hurdle. It is a ranking heuristic, not a probability
that a strategy works. Funding separately requires positive uncertainty-adjusted
peer-relative return effects, sample coverage and portfolio risk feasibility;
ownership is not funding. Calibrate effect magnitudes from settled observations;
never treat this dimensionless score as expected return or require profitable
standalone trading P&L as proof of prediction. Evaluate long and short selection
separately from any passive hedge. In full robustness mode the supplied predefined
checks also include issuer-group stability, disjoint forward blocks, within-peer
shuffled-signal controls and genuine issuer-OOF where available. An issuer-OOF fold
must exclude its held-out issuers from all training history. Unavailable checks
are disclosed, not silently counted as passes. The fixed-candidate four-arm
comparison retains the basic four checks above so it isolates its stated changes.
